Use the following curated metadata and promotional copy when publishing **Remote Trackpad Pro** on the **Google Play Console** ([play.google.com/console](https://play.google.com/console)).

## 1. Store Listing Details

### App Title (30 characters max)
```text
Remote Trackpad Pro
```

### Short Description (80 characters max)
```text
Turn your phone into a MacBook glass trackpad, pro gamepad & mechanical keyboard
```

- **Tags**: `Remote Mouse`, `Gamepad`, `Trackpad`, `PC Controller`, `Keyboard`, `Touchpad`, `Wireless Mouse`
- **Content Rating**: `Everyone (3+)`

## 2. Full Description (4000 characters max)

```markdown
🖱️ Transform your Android smartphone or tablet into a high-precision MacBook-Grade Glass Trackpad, Dual-Layout Gaming Controller, and Authentic 80% Mechanical Keyboard for your PC!

Remote Trackpad Pro is an ultra-low-latency, zero-driver wireless peripheral engineered for seamless desktop control, gaming, and productivity over your local Wi-Fi network.

🌟 KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
• 100% Offline & Private: Operates strictly over your local Wi-Fi (LAN). Zero cloud servers, telemetry, or third-party trackers.
• Zero Kernel Drivers: Communicates directly via OS user-space input simulation. No unsigned driver warnings or anti-cheat conflicts.
• Sub-5ms Response Time: Hardware-accelerated touch processing with display-refresh batching delivers razor-sharp responsiveness.

---

✨ MAJOR FEATURES:

🖱️ 1. MACBOOK-GRADE MONOLITHIC GLASS TRACKPAD
• macOS Pointer Acceleration: Piecewise velocity ballistics allow single-pixel precision on slow moves and multi-monitor flicks on fast swipes.
• Integrated Click Strips: Dedicated Left & Right click zones with subtle laser divider.
• Thumb-and-Glide Dragging: Hold left click with one thumb while another finger glides to effortlessly select text or drag windows.
• Natural Inertial Scrolling: Two-finger natural swipe with continuous momentum decay.
• Multi-Touch Gestures: Two-finger tap for instantaneous Right Click; double-tap & hold for Drag Lock.

🎮 2. DUAL-LAYOUT PRO GAMEPAD CONSOLE
• Asymmetric (Xbox Style) & Symmetric (PlayStation Style) console layouts.
• Concentric Dual Thumbsticks: 360° virtual sticks with 18% deadzone filtering, 8-directional angular octant mapping (WASD / Diagonals), and outer sprint ring with tactile haptic confirmation.
• Clover Petal 4-Way D-Pad: Fast-actuating directional petals for fighting and retro games.
• Dynamic Button Text Engine: Switch in 1 tap between Button Names (A, B, X, Y), In-Game Actions (JUMP, CROUCH, FIRE, AIM), and PC Keys (SPACE, C, M1, M2).
• Live Rebinding: Long-press any button to customize its display label and rebind its PC key.

⌨️ 3. 80% TKL PHYSICAL MECHANICAL KEYBOARD
• Complete 87-Key Chassis: Function Row (Esc, F1–F12), dual-character Number row, QWERTY, Home row with Caps Lock LED, and Inverted-T arrow cluster.
• Cherry MX Switch Acoustics: Native SoundPool hardware audio synthesizes authentic mechanical switch clicks, stabilizer snaps, and spacebar thuds with zero delay.
• Smart Modifier Latching: Tap Shift, Ctrl, or Alt to effortlessly execute PC shortcuts like Ctrl + C, Ctrl + V, and Alt + Tab on your phone.
• Quick Text & Voice Dictation: Stream long messages or use Gboard speech-to-text to type directly onto your computer.

🎵 4. MEDIA & VOLUME CONTROLLER
• Dedicated cards for desktop playback (Play/Pause, Next, Prev) and audio adjustments (Vol +, Vol -, Mute).
```

## 3. Required Store Graphics Checklist

1. **App Icon**:
   - 512 × 512 PNG, 32-bit color, max 1MB.
2. **Feature Graphic**:
   - 1024 × 500 JPG or PNG, max 15MB. (You can use `assets/banner.jpg`!).
3. **Phone Screenshots**:
   - Minimum 2 screenshots, recommended 4–8.
   - You can upload the high-res screenshots already present in the `assets/` folder:
     - `assets/Trackpad .png`
     - `assets/Trackpad fullscreen.png`
     - `assets/Gamepad layout 1.png`
     - `assets/Gmepad layout 2.png`
     - `assets/Keyboard control.png`
     - `assets/media control.png`
4. **Privacy Policy URL**:
   - Link to `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` hosted on GitHub (e.g. `https://github.com/AgrShubham/remote_mouse/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`).
